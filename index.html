Linear Studio

One studio. Every game. Zero compromises.

Linear Studio was founded in late 2025 as the direct continuation of Zenite Services, a name many of you remember from the start. Zenite was established in early 2025 with a single focus: undetectable, high-performance cheats for Rainbow Six Siege. It earned a big and loyal following, built on one promise: software that holds, updates that land, and support that actually answers.

When the time came to grow, we didn't just rebrand the product, we rebuilt the house around it. Linear Studio carries Zenite's DNA forward and expands it across the entire competitive landscape: CS2, Valorant, Rust, Fortnite, Escape from Tarkov, Apex Legends and more, all under one roof, one loader, one lifetime model. The kernel engine is our own. The feature stack is our own. The update pipeline ships within hours of a patch, not days. Nothing is outsourced, nothing is rented, which is precisely why nothing leaks.

We're not the biggest storefront yet and that's intentional for now. We build what we ship, we ship what works, and we don't disappear after your payment clears.
